Alphabetical dictionary of ProofCash and zero-knowledge crypto terminology explained simply:
AIR (Algebraic Intermediate Representation) 1,842 Constraints

The set of mathematical polynomial equations verified by the zero-knowledge prover. While standard WebAuthn requires 350,000 equations (taking 8+ seconds and heating up your phone), ProofCash Two-Tier uses only 1,842 equations, completing in < 45 milliseconds!

eUTXO (Extended Unspent Transaction Output) Digital Paper Cash

Unlike Ethereum accounts that lock your whole account while a transfer is pending, eUTXOs work exactly like paper dollar bills with serial numbers. You can spend multiple bills simultaneously in parallel without any congestion.

Nullifier & Commitment Privacy Shield

A Commitment is the cryptographic envelope that hides how much money you have. A Nullifier is the serial number published when you spend a coin to prevent double-spending without revealing your address.

Pico-Joules (pJ) 10⁻¹² Energy Unit

The smallest unit of ProofCash (1 PCASH = 1,000,000,000,000 pJ). Named after the physical unit of energy (joules) to reflect the thermodynamic compute underpinning the network.

PoSW (Proof-of-Succinct-Work) Fair Energy Mining

The consensus mechanism that mints new ProofCash. Unlike Bitcoin which requires massive industrial ASIC power plants, PoSW is optimized for consumer CPUs and phones using Blake3 SIMD instructions.
